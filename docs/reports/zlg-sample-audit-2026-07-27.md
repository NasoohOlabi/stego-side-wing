## Correction: our recoverable capacity is under-reported by 43%

`scripts/build_zlg_method_comparison_dataset.py:342-364` recomputes each row's capacity by
calling `selection_channel_capacity_report()` on `<run>/dataset/<post_id>.json`. That snapshot
is the pre-angle-generation post; it has **no `angles` key** (angles live in the sibling
`input-angles/` directory). The report therefore returns `tangent_choices: 0`, and line 359
overwrites `selection_bits` with comment-channel capacity alone. This happened on
**304 of 304 our-method rows**.

The sender artifacts show the angle channel was live in every sample —
`embedding.angleEmbedding.bitsCount` across the 54 artifacts is 4 bits ×2, 8 bits ×12,
9 bits ×31, 11 bits ×9, alongside 11-14 comment bits.

| Recoverable bits per carrier | row mean | post-clustered mean | median | min | max |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| As stored in the artifact (comment channel only) | 10.53 | **10.66** | 10 | 10 | 13 |
| Recomputed with the angle channel | 18.43 | **18.66** | 19 | 13 | 21 |
| Physical width actually consumed | 20.41 | 20.64 | 21 | 15 | 23 |

Worked example — post `1lpx80x`: 2914 comment choices, 439 angles →
`floor(log2 2914) + floor(log2 439) = 11 + 8 = 19` recoverable bits, stored as 11.

Consequences:

- The 2026-07-26 audit's headline row `Recoverable selection capacity 10.66 vs 76.70` should
  read **18.66 vs 76.70**. ZLG still leads on capacity, by ≈4.1x rather than ≈7.2x.
- `.agents/method-and-zlg-benchmark.md` and `/zlg-comparison` inherit the same figure.
- Nothing else changes: no quality, detection, or judge metric depends on this field.

The recomputation is deliberately conservative — it keeps `floor(log2 N)` per channel, so it
measures the same lossless-selection quantity the method claims, only without dropping a
channel.

Four properties of this run limit what may be claimed from it:

1. **Our arm has 47 unique texts across 304 rows.** Each text is re-scored ~6.5x, so every
   row-level statistic and judge call is correlated. Only post-clustered inference is valid.
2. **Our payload was not channel-recovered.** `payload_bytes_target = 66` and
   `audit_assisted_payload_bytes = 66` on all 304 rows, but the receiver was handed the
   sender's compressed bitstring. The sender artifacts carry the warning "Selection channel did
   not carry the full compressed payload" with a mean of **477 of ~492 compressed bits left
   unembedded** (post-clustered 477.06). Only `selection_bits` is real channel capacity.
3. **ZLG's payload was near-constant** — the tracking string
   `actual-e2e:20260711T173204Z:…` sliced into 10-byte chunks, 69 distinct chunks over 304
   accepted attempts — and its accepted rows survived a relaxed quality gate with up to 4
   retries, so its quality distribution is a survivor distribution.
4. **`quality_passed` is degenerate**: `True` on 100% of our rows, `False` on 100% of ZLG rows.
   It records which writer populated the field, not a quality outcome, and must not be surfaced
   as a result.

Encoding hygiene is clean in this run: the only non-ASCII in our texts is U+2014 (15 rows), and
there is no U+FFFD in any stego text, cover text, thread context, or reference. The
mojibake noted in the 2026-07-26 audit belongs to older remote samples, not to these.

## Sample-level defects in our outputs

**Templated openers.** Across the 47 unique texts: "why people keep …" in **15 (32%)**,
"keep coming back" 7, "keep bringing up" 5, "keep circling back" 2, "Honestly," 4,
"it feels like" 4. The same phrases appear 0-1 times in 304 ZLG texts. A warden holding two
of our carriers sees a recurring frame.

**Orphan fragments from the selection channel.** Several outputs open with a fragment lifted
from the selected comment or angle before the reply begins:

> *"Poll is about choosing between three popular coffee shops. I can see why people keep coming
> back to that point …"* — on a thread about the Diddy verdict.

> *"highlighting over $1 billion in transactions from 2003 to 2019 involving various companies,
> individuals, and Russian banks. It's wild how …"* — lowercase sentence start (3.3% of rows).

**Tangent grafting can break coherence.** Worst case in the run:
*"I feel frustrated seeing science budgets slashed even as basic site requirements like
essential cookies get more important for user trust"* — GPT-2 perplexity 626, the run maximum.

**Structural monotony.** 54.9% of our outputs are single-sentence, which is what makes
words-per-sentence separable from human comments at AUC 0.800 (table below).

The judges agree with the grounding complaint: G-Eval relevance 1.924 vs ZLG 2.368
(sign-test p=0.002 in the 07-26 clustering), while thread-grounded factuality favours us,
1.783 vs 1.564. Both arms sit near the judge's floor (~1.8/5 overall), so the judge's own
calibration is a caveat, not a result.

## Structural property to disclose in the security discussion

`compress_payload` emits the compression-method flag as the first bit of the compressed stream
(`src/workflows/utils/stego_codec.py:509-528`), and `embed_in_comment_selection` consumes the
leading bits as the comment index. Under the default `plain` payload transform, the **most
significant bit of the comment index is therefore the compression flag**: standard mode pushes
the reply into the lower half of the comment list, dictionary mode into the upper half.
Independently, the index is uniform over up to 9138 comments (mean 2364 in this run), whereas
real repliers concentrate on top-level, recent, high-score comments.

Both are aggregate-detectable by a warden who holds the thread. Neither invalidates a
single-carrier result, but the paper must state them, and the fix — keyed whitening of the
bitstream plus a prior-weighted selection decoder — belongs in the limitations discussion.

## Recommended next work

Ordered by impact per unit of effort. None of these were executed as part of this audit.

1. Fix the capacity enrichment defect: resolve the post from
   `output-results/<post>_*.json[0]["post"]` (which carries `angles`) or merge
   `input-angles/<post_id>.json`, and refuse to write a row where `tangent_choices == 0` while
   `angleEmbedding.bitsCount > 0`. Re-emit `paired_rows.jsonl` and `summary.json`.
2. Surface `recovery_source` beside every capacity number in `/zlg-comparison`, retire
   `quality_passed`, and resolve the BERTScore provenance contradiction: this run's
   `METRICS_RUN_PROGRESS.md` says `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (roberta-large download failed) while
   `paired_rows.jsonl` provenance says `roberta-large`. No BERTScore number should be published
   until one of the two is shown stale.
3. Code the comment and angle choices as a single joint index (arithmetic/range coding) instead
   of two truncated fixed-width fields with modulo folding
   (`stego_codec.py:615-649`, `:698-716`). Capacity becomes `log2(comment × angle)` — a further
   1-4 bits per carrier here — with no change to the visible text.
4. Decode payload bits against a non-uniform comment prior (depth, recency, score, sibling rank)
   and whiten the payload with a keyed transform. Capacity drops to the entropy of that prior,
   but the aggregate tells above disappear.
5. Add acceptance-side fingerprint gates in `src/workflows/pipelines/stego_candidates.py` /
   `stego_contextuality.py`: rolling opener n-gram blocklist, orphan-fragment and
   copied-span rejection, sentence-count band. Acceptance-side only, so no workflow prompt
   changes are involved; every rejection must be counted as a method failure.
6. Index comments by stable id rather than list position, so a comment added or deleted between
   send and receive does not shift every index in a 2000-comment thread.
7. Rerun with `scripts/run_publication_benchmark.py --comparison-mode capacity_matched` on the
   frozen manifest: one independent generation per trial, equal useful payload, blind decode
   (`compressed_full=None`, `strict_decode=True`), failures counted as zero, length-matched
   KL/JSD with held-out human controls. Before that run, reconcile the client defaults in
   `src/services/zlg_comparison_service.py` (temperature 1.0, alpha 1.25,
   `FRAMING_HEADER_BITS=16`) with the deployed server's (0.7, 1.0, headerless).
